# World Rules — Verdant Earth
*Permanent rules. Any book that contradicts these has an error.*

## The Ecological Covenant (2051)
- Global treaty following the Cascade Collapse of 2047–2049 (simultaneous failure of three major ocean current systems)
- Prohibits all new fossil fuel extraction; requires full phase-out by 2065
- Establishes Restoration Credits: economic units tied to measurable ecological recovery (carbon sequestration, biodiversity indices, ocean pH)
- Enforced by the Accord (successor to the UN, founded 2053)
- Not universally loved: some regions feel it was imposed by wealthy nations who caused the problem

## Governance: The Accord
- Voluntary cooperative of city-states and bioregional councils
- No standing army; enforcement is economic (credit sanctions)
- Three bodies: Technical Council (scientific standards), Trade Assembly (economic coordination), Rights Tribunal (human rights and ecological harm adjudication)
- Real power is often at the city-state and bioregional level

## Biotech Integration
- Living infrastructure is standard in Accord cities: mycorrhizal data networks (fungal fiber optics), biofilter wetlands treating water and air, mycelial composting systems integrated into building waste
- All biotech systems are certified by the Technical Council; uncertified modifications are illegal under the Covenant
- "Going wild" — running unsanctioned biotech — carries serious legal consequences
- Biotech systems are not infallible; they require maintenance, can be sabotaged, and sometimes fail in cascading ways

## Energy
- Decentralized renewable grid; no single point of failure by design
- Personal and community energy ownership is the norm (not utility monopolies)
- Energy is not scarce in Accord cities, but it is tracked (for Restoration Credit accounting)

## Technology
- AI is ubiquitous but narrow; general AI did not happen — systems are highly capable within domains but do not generalize
- No brain-computer interfaces (banned under the Rights Tribunal's 2068 Body Sovereignty ruling)
- Physical infrastructure has been significantly rebuilt; most 20th-century heavy industry has been replaced or converted

## What the Cascade Collapse left behind
- Sea level is 0.8m higher than 2000; many coastal cities rebuilt on elevated footings or with flood-adaptive design
- Several island nations are gone or greatly diminished; their populations resettled into coastal cooperatives
- Certain ecosystems (Great Barrier Reef, large sections of the Amazon) are in managed recovery but not restored
- A generation born after 2060 has never known a world without the Covenant; older generations remember the Before Time with a mix of grief and relief

## Economics
- Restoration Credits are the dominant global currency for large transactions; local currencies for daily commerce
- Intellectual property is governed by the Open Knowledge Compact (2058): most scientific and technical knowledge is freely accessible; commercial advantage comes from implementation, not information hoarding
- Scarcity economics still applies to physical goods and skilled labor; post-scarcity is not here yet
