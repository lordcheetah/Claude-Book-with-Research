# World Rules — Verdant Earth
*Permanent rules. Any book that contradicts these has an error.*

## The Ecological Covenant (2051)
- Global treaty following the Cascade Collapse of 2047–2049 (simultaneous failure of three major ocean current systems)
- Prohibits all new fossil fuel extraction; requires full phase-out by 2065
- Establishes Restoration Credits: economic units tied to measurable ecological recovery (carbon sequestration, biodiversity indices, ocean pH)
- Enforced by the Accord (successor to the UN, founded 2053)
- Not universally loved: some regions feel it was imposed by wealthy nations who caused the problem

## Governance: The Accord
- Voluntary cooperative of city-states and bioregional councils
- No standing army; enforcement is economic (credit sanctions)
- Three bodies: Technical Council (scientific standards), Trade Assembly (economic coordination), Rights Tribunal (human rights and ecological harm adjudication)
- Real power is often at the city-state and bioregional level

## Biotech Integration
- Living infrastructure is standard in Accord cities: mycorrhizal data networks (fungal fiber optics), biofilter wetlands treating water and air, mycelial composting systems integrated into building waste
- All biotech systems are certified by the Technical Council; uncertified modifications are illegal under the Covenant
- "Going wild" — running unsanctioned biotech — carries serious legal consequences
- Biotech systems are not infallible; they require maintenance, can be sabotaged, and sometimes fail in cascading ways

## Energy
- Decentralized renewable grid; no single point of failure by design
- Personal and community energy ownership is the norm (not utility monopolies)
- Energy is not scarce in Accord cities, but it is tracked (for Restoration Credit accounting)

## Technology
- AI is ubiquitous but narrow; general AI did not happen — systems are highly capable within domains but do not generalize
- No brain-computer interfaces (banned under the Rights Tribunal's 2068 Body Sovereignty ruling)
- Physical infrastructure has been significantly rebuilt; most 20th-century heavy industry has been replaced or converted

## What the Cascade Collapse left behind
- Sea level is 0.8m higher than 2000; many coastal cities rebuilt on elevated footings or with flood-adaptive design
- Several island nations are gone or greatly diminished; their populations resettled into coastal cooperatives
- Certain ecosystems (Great Barrier Reef, large sections of the Amazon) are in managed recovery but not restored
- A generation born after 2060 has never known a world without the Covenant; older generations remember the Before Time with a mix of grief and relief

## Economics
- Restoration Credits are the dominant global currency for large transactions; local currencies for daily commerce
- Intellectual property is governed by the Open Knowledge Compact (2058): most scientific and technical knowledge is freely accessible; commercial advantage comes from implementation, not information hoarding
- Scarcity economics still applies to physical goods and skilled labor; post-scarcity is not here yet

## NEW RULES ESTABLISHED — Book 1 (The Mycelial City)
*The following are now PROVEN, demonstrated facts about how this world's institutions and systems function. Any future book set in this world must reckon with them as precedent — not as untested theory.*

### The Open Knowledge Compact — now a PROVEN, demonstrated mechanism (not just a founding-era provision)
- The Compact (2058) grants a permanent, separate **public right of access** to ecological monitoring data — distinct from, and not routed through, any institution's internal reporting chain (not the incident queue, not the Technical Council, not any body that "can make a thing disappear").
- **Source**: Book 1, Chapters 4–6. Yara Osei used her personal Compact deposit credentials to publish the complete sabotage record directly to the public archive when every official channel had been shut to her.
- **What is now proven, on the page, for the first time in the series**: the mechanism *works exactly as designed* — independent strangers across the cooperative network began pulling and verifying the deposited record within nineteen minutes (the first reader was a researcher in the Nairobi bioregion); a "string of single, real, separate eyes" arrived independently at the same conclusion. A public deposit triggers an **automated, mandatory Technical Council notification** — "a mechanism [that] noticed on its own and... can't pretend it didn't" — which in turn triggered a real, fast, and largely uncontrollable institutional cascade (contract freezes, a formal certification review, and — within the same morning — a resignation).
- **Permanent precedent for future books**: this is no longer a theoretical "load-bearing founding commitment." It is now a *demonstrated, working, fast-acting* check on institutional capture — one that any future saboteur, corrupt official, or captured institution in this world must now treat as a known, provable risk. Likewise, any future protagonist locked out of official channels now has on-record proof that this door exists, has been used, and works.
- **Standing requirement** (now confirmed in practice): Compact deposit access is tied to *the work itself* — certified labor on shared environmental/public-infrastructure systems — not to any employer, assignment, or institutional standing. It is issued by the Covenant directly to the individual and cannot be revoked by an employer (an Authority suspension could not touch it).

### Credential / access-tier distinction — now fully established series-canon
- **Source**: Book 1, Chapters 4–6.
- New Accra's (and by extension, Accord-city) institutional access runs on at least two entirely separate credential systems with **separate failure modes**:
  1. **Authority/employer operational credentials** — assignment-based, can be suspended or frozen by institutional action (in Book 1, within four minutes of an attempted formal escalation, via a "unified registry" that links Authority and Accord secondment credentials together — one suspension action reached both Yara's and Del's access simultaneously).
  2. **Compact deposit / public-infrastructure-work credentials** — issued directly by the Covenant to individuals based on the nature of their work, entirely separate from any employer's authority to grant or revoke, and explicitly NOT reachable by an institutional suspension.
- This distinction is now load-bearing series-canon: future books can use either failure mode (or the gap between them) as a plot mechanism, but must not conflate the two systems or treat one as able to override the other.

### Manual biofilter override — now an established mechanical/procedural fact
- **Source**: Book 1, Chapter 6.
- Biofilter stations have a **manual override mechanism** that is purely mechanical, requires a physical key, and "answers to no credential at all." It requires only **"access and competence"** — meaning a suspended or credential-locked technician can still physically operate the system if they have the training and the access (which a suspension does not revoke, since station housings answer to badges/physical access, not operational assignment status).
- This establishes that New Accra's (and likely all Covenant-certified) biotech infrastructure has a deliberate **human-competence fallback layer** beneath its credentialed/automated systems — a designed redundancy, not a loophole. Future books should treat this as intentional resilience design baked into Covenant-era infrastructure.

### The suppression "kill threshold" — now an established ecological/technical rule
- **Source**: Book 1, Chapter 6.
- Biofilter colony suppression-response cannot be forced past a certain intensity without killing the colonies themselves — manual remediation must be paced to "what the living system can bear." Pushing the suppression dose too hard "overcorrects" and kills the very organisms being relied upon to fight the bloom.
- This is now a permanent rule of how mycorrhizal/biofilter biotech functions in this world: remediation is inherently rate-limited by the biology itself, not just by procedure or equipment. Any future book depicting biofilter remediation, sabotage, or recovery must respect this ceiling.

### Firmware/certification sabotage — a demonstrated method, now precedent
- **Source**: Book 1, Chapters 2–4.
- It is now established as *possible and proven* that a single, narrow, deliberate firmware modification can run identically across multiple manufacturers' hardware by operating at the monitoring-software layer (above and indifferent to the underlying machine) — meaning hardware diversity alone is NOT a defense against this class of sabotage.
- Such a modification can be installed during a legitimate-seeming certification site visit, with the certification itself serving as the cover and the paper trail. This makes the **certification process itself** a documented attack vector in this world — a permanent vulnerability class that future books may reference, reuse, or show being patched.
- The "selective substitution" technique (passing all data through accurately except one specific signal range, which is replaced with a synthesized nominal value) is now an established sabotage signature — subtle enough to look, on first inspection, like an aggregation/processing bug rather than deliberate interference.
