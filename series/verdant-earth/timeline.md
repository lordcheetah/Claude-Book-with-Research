# Series Timeline — Verdant Earth

## Before the Series: Key Historical Events

- **2047–2049**: The Cascade Collapse — simultaneous failure of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation, parts of the Antarctic ice shelf, and three major monsoon systems. Mass displacement, crop failure, political collapse in several regions.
- **2051**: Ecological Covenant signed in Nairobi by 147 nations and city-states.
- **2053**: The Accord founded as successor governance body.
- **2057**: Yara Osei born in Kumasi Cooperative (Ghana).
- **2058**: Open Knowledge Compact; scientific data becomes a global commons.
- **2065**: Fossil fuel phase-out deadline reached in Accord territories.
- **2068**: Body Sovereignty ruling bans brain-computer interfaces.
- **2080s**: First generation raised entirely under the Covenant reaches adulthood; the "Covenant children."
- **2088**: New Accra (West African coast) achieves Restoration Credit surplus for the first time — a major symbolic milestone.
- **2091**: Book 1 begins.
