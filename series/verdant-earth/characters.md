## Yara Osei
*(protagonist of Book 1: The Mycelial City)*

### Permanent facts
- Age at series start: 34 (born 2057, grew up in the resettlement generation)
- Background: Born in the Kumasi Cooperative to parents who relocated from flooded coastal areas; grew up with the Covenant as simply the way the world worked
- Expertise: Water systems engineering; specializes in biofilter networks
- Core traits: Methodical, skeptical of easy answers, protective of the systems she maintains, dry humor under pressure

## The Accord Technical Council
*Institution — recurring background presence*

- The standards body for all biotech certification in Accord cities
- Tends toward conservatism: slow to certify new systems, fast to investigate failures
- Has genuine scientific integrity but is susceptible to political pressure from major city-states
- First appears in Book 1 as the body that must certify Yara's findings
