# Verdant Earth — Series Overview

## What this series is
Near-future Earth (2090s–2110s) after the Great Restoration — humanity's incomplete but real recovery from the climate crisis. Cities are bio-integrated and renewable, ecological governance has replaced most nation-states, and a generation has grown up believing the worst is behind them. Each book follows a different character navigating the tensions of a world that is healing but not healed.

## Tone and genre
Solarpunk: grounded optimism. The world is genuinely better than the 21st century — not utopia, but real progress made at real cost. Conflicts arise from human nature, institutional inertia, and the gap between ideals and implementation — not from the world being secretly terrible. Thriller pacing, literary attention to character and place.

## Target audience
Adult readers who want science fiction that imagines good futures without naivety. Fans of Kim Stanley Robinson, Becky Chambers, and Ursula K. Le Guin.

## Scope
- Books planned: 2 (test series — open to extension)
- Time span: 2091–2097
- Geographic focus: the West African coast and global cooperative networks

## Core premise
The Ecological Covenant (2051) saved the world — but saving the world created winners and losers, and not everyone agrees on what "saved" means. Each book is a standalone mystery/thriller set within the same world, featuring different protagonists connected by a shared universe.

## What makes this series distinct
The conflicts are not "will civilization survive" but "who controls what kind of civilization." The antagonists are institutions and incentives, not villains. The protagonists solve real, technical problems using expertise, cooperation, and genuine care for the world.
