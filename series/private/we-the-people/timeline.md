# Series Timeline — We The People

## Key events across the series

### Pre-series background (before 1765)
- 1732: George Washington born, Westmoreland County, Virginia
- 1735: John Adams born, Braintree, Massachusetts
- 1737: Thomas Paine born, Thetford, Norfolk, England
- 1743: Thomas Jefferson born, Shadwell, Virginia
- 1744: Abigail Smith (Adams) born, Weymouth, Massachusetts
- 1706: Benjamin Franklin born, Boston (eldest figure — 59 at start of series)
- 1751: James Madison born, Port Conway, Virginia
- 1754–1763: French and Indian War (Seven Years War) — Washington's military formation
- 1756: Franklin goes to London as Pennsylvania's colonial agent
- 1764: John Adams marries Abigail Smith

### Book 1 events: The Road to Independence (1765–1776)
- **1765**: Stamp Act passed by Parliament; colonial uproar; Stamp Act Congress
- **1767**: Townshend Acts; colonial boycotts
- **1770**: Boston Massacre (March 5); Adams defends British soldiers
- **1772**: Franklin exposed as leak in the Hutchinson Letters affair in London
- **1773**: Boston Tea Party (December 16)
- **1774**: Intolerable Acts; First Continental Congress (September–October)
- **1774**: Thomas Paine arrives in Philadelphia (November), with letter from Franklin
- **1775**: Lexington and Concord (April 19); Second Continental Congress convenes (May)
- **1775**: Washington appointed Commander in Chief (June 15)
- **1775**: Paine begins writing Common Sense
- **1776**: Common Sense published (January 10) — massive public impact
- **1776**: Abigail Adams writes "Remember the Ladies" letter (March 31)
- **1776**: Congress votes for independence (July 2); Declaration signed (August 2, most signatures)
- **1776**: Jefferson's draft includes anti-slavery clause; Congress strikes it

### Book 2 events: The Long War (1776–1783)
- **1776**: Battle of Trenton (December 26); Paine writes "The Crisis"
- **1777**: Valley Forge winter (1777–78); near-collapse of Continental Army
- **1777**: Battle of Saratoga — turning point
- **1778**: French alliance signed; Franklin the architect
- **1779**: Jefferson becomes Governor of Virginia
- **1781**: Cornwallis surrenders at Yorktown (October 19)
- **1782**: Martha Jefferson dies (September 6)
- **1783**: Treaty of Paris signed (September 3); Franklin, Adams, Jay negotiate

### Book 3 events: A More Perfect Union (1783–1789)
- **1784**: Jefferson appointed Minister to France
- **1785**: Adams appointed Minister to Britain
- **1786**: Shays' Rebellion in Massachusetts — exposes weakness of Articles
- **1787**: Constitutional Convention in Philadelphia (May–September); Madison's Virginia Plan
- **1787–88**: Federalist Papers published (Hamilton, Madison, Jay as "Publius")
- **1788**: Constitution ratified (June); Franklin dies April 1790 (just after this period)
- **1789**: First elections; Washington unanimously elected President

### Book 4 events: The First Republic (1789–1797)
- **1789**: Washington's inauguration (April 30); Adams as Vice President
- **1789**: Hamilton appointed Secretary of Treasury; Jefferson as Secretary of State
- **1790**: Franklin dies (April 17)
- **1790–91**: Hamilton's financial program; debt assumption; national bank
- **1791**: Bill of Rights ratified (December 15)
- **1791**: Jefferson resigns as Secretary of State; returns to Monticello (end of 1793)
- **1793**: Washington's Neutrality Proclamation (French Revolutionary Wars)
- **1794**: Whiskey Rebellion
- **1796**: Washington's Farewell Address; Adams elected 2nd President
- **1797**: Adams inaugurated; series ends
