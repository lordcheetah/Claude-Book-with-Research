# Chapter Plan — The Manifest Anomaly

## Chapter 1: The Numbers Don't Resolve
**Function**: Setup / Inciting incident
**Location**: Operations floor, Deck 2
**POV focus**: Maya Chen
**Events**:
- Introduce Maya at work; establish her deliberate invisibility and methodical nature
- The manifest anomaly surfaces during routine processing
- She files the standard report — and notes, without alarm, that no automated confirmation comes back
**Ends with**: She goes to sleep. The report goes nowhere.

## Chapter 2: Correction Applied
**Function**: Complication
**Location**: Operations floor → Maya's quarters
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya finds her anomaly report "corrected" — figures revised with no log entry
- Queries the revision; system shows nothing anomalous
- Vasquez calls her in for a casual-seeming clarification meeting; he knows too much
**Ends with**: She returns to her desk. She starts a personal log that exists nowhere on station systems.

## Chapter 3: What the Weight Sensors Know
**Function**: Rising action / Investigation begins
**Location**: Warrens, Deck 9–10; Data center entrance
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya begins pulling physical data (weight sensors, environmental logs) rather than manifest systems
- Pattern emerging: systematic over-weight on specific cargo containers, six prior shipments
- She encounters Devlin in the warrens; he's been tracking the same anomalies from the physical side, told to "leave it"
**Ends with**: They decide not to trust each other yet — but they share a data point.

## Chapter 4: Eighteen Months
**Function**: Rising action / Scope established
**Location**: Data center (Deck 11), The Hub
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Deep dive into historical data: the diversion has been running for 18 months, pre-dating current station management rotation
- Compound identification: three chemicals with one viable combined use (industrial, not commercial)
- She realizes the cover-up requires active cooperation from at least three departments
**Ends with**: She names the compounds in her personal log. She knows what they're for.

## Chapter 5: The Helpful Engineer
**Function**: Rising action / Alliance forming
**Location**: Deck 7 engineering, The Hub
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya and Devlin establish trust through a shared data comparison that independently confirms each other's findings
- Devlin's perspective: he was told the weight discrepancies were "sensor calibration issues" — the calibration logs were then altered
- They discuss who can be trusted; Commander Park is an unknown quantity
**Ends with**: They agree on a protocol: separate work, shared dead drop, no direct communication on station systems.

## Chapter 6: The Director's View
**Function**: Rising action / Antagonist POV
**Location**: Deck 1 (restricted), external comms
**POV focus**: Vasquez
**Events**:
- Vasquez's perspective on the operation: his motives, his justifications, the buyer's demands
- He reviews Maya's file; she's flagged as "procedurally cautious, unlikely to escalate beyond standard channels"
- He gives an order that will compromise one more person in the chain
**Ends with**: He believes the problem is managed. He is wrong about Maya.

## Chapter 7: What Park Knows
**Function**: Rising action / Ally uncertainty
**Location**: Operations floor, Commander Park's office
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya attempts to gauge Park's involvement through an indirect conversation
- Park's response is carefully neutral — she knows something, but Maya can't determine what
- Maya is briefly surveilled; she notices and adapts
**Ends with**: She decides Park is not an ally she can use yet. She needs more.

## Chapter 8: The Storm Window
**Function**: Rising action / Clock established
**Location**: Weather monitoring systems, warrens
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Weather forecast: 72-hour storm window in 9 days; communications degraded, transport suspended
- Devlin identifies that the next shipment aligns exactly with the storm window — this isn't a coincidence
- They calculate what the storm window means: Vasquez will run the transfer under communications blackout
**Ends with**: They have a deadline.

## Chapter 9: Pressure
**Function**: Complications / Escalation
**Location**: Operations floor, Maya's quarters
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya's system access is subtly restricted — she finds three data paths she previously had that are now closed
- She realizes she's being monitored more closely than she thought
- A colleague she likes is transferred off-station with 24 hours notice; likely because they spoke to her
**Ends with**: Devlin sends a dead-drop message: "They're watching both of us now."

## Chapter 10: What He's Protecting
**Function**: Complications / Antagonist humanized
**Location**: Deck 1, personal quarters
**POV focus**: Vasquez
**Events**:
- Vasquez receives news from shore: the buyer has accelerated the timeline
- His personal history: the reason he agreed to this, the person he's protecting, the point of no return he passed two years ago
- He gives the order to surveil Devlin
**Ends with**: He tells himself one more transfer and it's over. He has told himself this before.

## Chapter 11: The Physical Evidence
**Function**: Complications / Key discovery
**Location**: Storage warrens, Deck 12
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Devlin, using his legitimate maintenance access, locates the physical containers — mislabeled, stored in an unlisted zone
- They cannot move them, but they photograph and document the serial numbers
- The serial numbers trace to a procurement order that doesn't match any station project
**Ends with**: They have physical evidence. It means nothing if they can't get it to someone who can act on it.

## Chapter 12: Channels
**Function**: Complications / Official route fails
**Location**: Communications room, Operations floor
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya attempts to use the standard regulatory reporting channel — discovers it routes through Vasquez's department for "local review" before going external
- The external audit system she needs is on Deck 11; her access was revoked three days ago
- She finds a legal clause: the external audit system must accept a direct submission from any station employee in cases of suspected financial crime
**Ends with**: She has a path. It requires being in the data center for 20 minutes with Vasquez's system admin lockout bypassed.

## Chapter 13: Devlin's Risk
**Function**: Complications / Stakes personal
**Location**: The Hub, warrens
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Devlin's access to the warrens is suspended pending a "safety review" — he's been identified
- He gives Maya the physical documentation before he's locked out
- They disagree on the next step: Devlin wants to confront Park directly; Maya refuses
**Ends with**: Devlin does it anyway — off-page. Maya doesn't know yet.

## Chapter 14: What Park Does
**Function**: Complications / Pivot
**Location**: Commander Park's office, Deck 2
**POV focus**: Vasquez
**Events**:
- Park meets with Vasquez after Devlin's approach — but doesn't report Devlin's information
- Instead, she does something small and undisclosed that creates a 6-hour window in the data center access logs
- We understand that Park is not complicit but is complicit-adjacent — she knows and is managing the situation in her own way
**Ends with**: The window exists. Maya doesn't know it yet.

## Chapter 15: The Storm Begins
**Function**: Climax / Clock running
**Location**: Station, all decks
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Storm conditions lock down Deck 1–3; communications degraded
- Maya discovers the 6-hour window Park created — she doesn't know who created it
- She enters the data center using Devlin's maintenance override (which hasn't been revoked yet — an oversight)
**Ends with**: She's in. 20 minutes.

## Chapter 16: 20 Minutes
**Function**: Climax / Central confrontation
**Location**: Data center, Deck 11
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Maya works against the clock to compile and push the audit submission
- Vasquez arrives — also running an operation (destroying evidence)
- Neither of them expected the other; the confrontation is not physical but a race between two data operations on adjacent terminals
**Ends with**: The submission goes through. The evidence destruction is 70% complete. It's a partial win.

## Chapter 17: Aftermath
**Function**: Resolution
**Location**: Operations floor, medical bay, communications
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- The regulatory flag triggers an external hold on the shipment — it doesn't sail in the storm window
- Vasquez is suspended; the investigation is opened
- Devlin is fine — Park protected him from formal action
- Maya learns what Park did (and didn't do), and accepts the ambiguity
**Ends with**: Storm passes. Maya files a full personal account with the external regulator. It will take months to resolve.

## Chapter 18: Not Your Problem
**Function**: Resolution / Epilogue
**Location**: Deck 2, transport departure
**POV focus**: Maya
**Events**:
- Two months later; Maya's contract ends
- The investigation is ongoing; Vasquez is on administrative leave, not yet charged
- Lily's treatments are covered through the end of the year; after that, uncertain
- Maya doesn't take another station posting
**Ends with**: She leaves Station Alpha. On the transport, she opens a new data file. She doesn't know what she's looking for yet.
