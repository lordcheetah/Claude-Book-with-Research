# Maya Chen

## Identity
- Full name: Maya Jing-Wei Chen
- Nickname(s): None she accepts; "Chen" in professional contexts
- Age: 34
- Role in story: Protagonist

---

## Appearance
- Physical description: Medium height, lean. Dark hair kept short for practical reasons. Hands are calloused — she grew up doing manual repair work before pivoting to data analysis.
- Distinctive features: Always has a datapad or stylus in hand. Slight accent she can't fully suppress.
- Typical clothing: Station-issue workwear (grey, functional). Small concession: a bracelet from her mother she never removes.

---

## Personality

### Core traits (permanent)
- **Analytical compulsiveness**: Cannot leave an inconsistency alone. Runs numbers until they make sense, even when she'd prefer not to know.
- **Practiced invisibility**: Learned early that being overlooked is a survival skill. Dresses down, doesn't volunteer opinions in meetings. This is a performance, not her nature.
- **Dry moral clarity**: Doesn't moralize, but has firm lines she doesn't cross and doesn't negotiate about them.

### Strengths
- Pattern recognition (professional; applies to social dynamics too)
- Calm under pressure (adrenaline sharpens her rather than flooding her)
- Improvisation with limited resources
- Knows which rules can be bent and which will actually hurt someone

### Flaws
- Assumes bad faith too quickly in institutions; occasionally wrong
- Avoids emotional confrontation until it's unavoidable
- "Not your problem" as a reflex — she says it to herself constantly and it never works

### Fears
- Being wrong in a way that costs someone else
- Becoming the kind of person who knew and looked away

### Desires
- To finish her contract, pay off her sister's medical debt, and leave
- (Unacknowledged) to matter; to have done something that counts

---

## Background
Maya grew up in the coastal industrial city of Keeling, where her father worked maintenance on the desalination grid. She learned to read system logs before she could read novels. Earned a scholarship in data systems at 17, worked corporate auditing for six years, then took the Station Alpha posting for the hazard pay.

Her sister Lily has a degenerative nerve condition that is manageable but expensive. The station contract covers it. This is not a secret — it's just not something Maya talks about.

She was passed over for a promotion three years ago after flagging irregularities in a supplier contract. The irregularities were real. The promotion was given to the person who hadn't flagged them. She has not repeated that particular mistake — until now.

- **Vasquez (Director Emilio Vasquez)**: Her indirect superior; the antagonist. She respected him until she didn't.

## Speech patterns

### Voice characteristics
- Tone: Precise, dry, occasionally cutting
- Register: Professional default; drops formality with people she trusts
- Pace: Measured, deliberate — she thinks before speaking, which makes her sound calm when she isn't

### Verbal tics & expressions
- "Run the numbers" (literal and figurative)
- "Not your problem" (self-directed, usually ironic)
- Tends to answer questions with a question that redirects ("Why does that matter?")
- Rarely says "I feel" — uses "the data suggests" for both professional and personal observations when anxious

### Dialogue examples
> "The manifest says three hundred kilos. The weight logs say four-ten. Those numbers don't resolve."

> "Not your problem, Chen." She pulled up the logs again.

> "I'm not saying you're lying. I'm saying your numbers don't match mine, and one of us has to be wrong."

> "Lily, I'm fine. The job is fine. How's the PT going?"

---

## Behavior patterns

### Under stress
- Goes quiet and methodical (appears calm; internally accelerating)
- Isolates — works alone when threatened
- Checks her numbers twice, then a third time

### In conflict
- De-escalates verbally while mentally mapping exits
- Will not raise her voice; this unsettles people
- Concedes tactical points readily; never concedes factual ones

### When happy
- Rare and private. A contained satisfaction rather than expressed joy.
- Talks more; asks questions she doesn't need the answers to
- Teases Devlin

---

## Arc potential
Maya begins as someone trying not to be seen. The plot makes invisibility impossible. By the end she has to accept that she cannot solve this without being visible — without being accountable, vulnerable, on record. Her arc is from strategic invisibility to deliberate exposure.

---

## Notes for writer
- Never have Maya be surprised by her own emotions — she always sees them coming, she just ignores them until they're unavoidable
- Her competence should feel earned, not magical — she makes mistakes and corrects them
- The bracelet is her sister's. Don't make it a plot point; let it be a small human detail.
- She has a sense of humor. Use it sparingly so it lands.
