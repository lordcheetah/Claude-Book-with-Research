## Chapter 6: The Open Record — Completed (FINAL CHAPTER OF BOOK 1)

**Story date**: Day 4, dawn through evening (Hour ~92 elapsed → ~Hour 99-100, bloom contained with ~20h of margin preserved before chemical irreversibility at Hour 114).

**Summary**: The armed deposit fires on schedule at 5 AM — a quiet, anticlimactic status change that nonetheless cannot be un-seen. Within minutes, independent verifiers across the world begin pulling and confirming the record; within hours, the Technical Council logs automated notification, Del's secondment is quietly restored, and the institutional cascade begins. Yara chooses the inlet over watching the institutional response unfold — she walks the manual biofilter override by hand, station by station, and bends the bloom curve back under her own competence while no one is watching. Del arrives at midday with the full report: Altera's contracts frozen, the certification review opened with Strand's signature in the record, Rational Innovation's denial, and — landing hardest — Director Nwosu's resignation, which confirms the *shape* of Yara's private inference without ever becoming a confession. Walking back, Del closes the Altera-director-identity thread by handing it to the Council's investigation, and gives his own Technist-connection thread one small, bounded, unspoken beat. Alone at day's end, Yara returns to Station Seven, runs the same sensor check that opened the book, and finds — for the first time — that the smell and the numbers agree. She makes the log entry, breathes the station's air, and walks home. Book 1 ends on a soft landing: the system doing exactly what it is supposed to do.
