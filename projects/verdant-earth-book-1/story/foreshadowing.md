## [F-01] — The Physical Water Samples

**Type**: Chekhov's gun
**Planted**: Chapter 1
**Payoff expected**: Chapter 2–3 (when the digital record is further compromised, the physical samples become the only uncorrupted evidence)
**Urgency**: HIGH
**Plant**: Yara takes three physical water samples from the Station Seven inlet outflow, labels them by hand ("EI-S7-2091-0605"), and stows them in her kit's cold pocket. The chapter plan explicitly marks these as "a plant: they will matter later when the digital record has been compromised." Lab results are pending at 24-hour turnaround.
**Expected payoff**: When the digital system is shown to be compromised (reports deleted, feeds cleaned), these physical samples are the one piece of evidence that exists outside the manipulated data infrastructure. They will confirm the real cyano levels and tie back to the original anomaly.
**Status**: OPEN

## [F-03] — Station Seven's Embedded Hardware Log Entry

**Type**: Chekhov's gun
**Planted**: Chapter 1
**Payoff expected**: Chapter 3–5 (when Yara is suspended and official digital channels are closed to her)
**Urgency**: MEDIUM
**Plant**: After discovering her report is gone, Yara opens "a new urgent flag directly on Station Seven's raw data log — the one action available to her that needed no submission queue and no aggregation feed and no one's review window." **Expected payoff**: The embedded station log entry — written directly to hardware, not through the Authority's incident management system — represents a trail that cannot be reached and deleted by whoever cleaned the queue. Likely to be key evidence when physical inspection of the station hardware occurs in Ch. 3, or when the full record is published via the Open Knowledge Compact in Ch. 6.

## [F-04] — The Anomaly Pattern Recurring for Three Weeks Unnoticed

**Type**: Setup
**Planted**: Chapter 1
**Payoff expected**: Chapter 2–4 (the historical depth of the cover-up)
**Urgency**: MEDIUM
**Plant**: Yara discovers the same cyano-up/coherence-nominal pairing appearing four times in the last three weeks of Station Seven's logs. ## [F-06] — Three Estuary-Adjacent Stations Affected (Not All Eight)

**Type**: Setup / Mystery
**Planted**: Chapter 1
**Payoff expected**: Chapter 3–4 (when the firmware modification vector is identified)
**Urgency**: MEDIUM
**Plant**: Of the eight eastern inlet stations, only stations 7, 8, and 9 — "the three closest to the outer estuary, the three that met the open sea first" — show the anomaly in both the raw data and the suppressed summary. The other five read clean in both.

The selectivity of which stations are affected points toward deliberate targeting rather than system-wide error — a key clue in Ch. 3 when the firmware modification is identified.

**Type**: Character
**Planted**: Chapter 1

The earlier framing of her dual nature makes this outcome feel earned rather than a betrayal twist.

## [F-09] — The Aggregation Algorithm as Cover Mechanism

**Type**: Mystery / Setup
**Planted**: Chapter 1
**Payoff expected**: Chapter 3 (mechanism fully identified)
**Urgency**: MEDIUM
**Plant**: Yara traces one anomalous reading through the aggregation algorithm step by step and watches the elevated cyano number "stop being elevated" — "Not deleted — reclassified, folded into the noise floor as if it had never been a signal." The "aggregation error" hypothesis will be ruled out as a red herring, establishing the true vector.

## [F-17] — The Colony's Silence (Flat Coherence Despite Rising Cyano)

**Type**: Mystery / Chekhov's gun
**Planted**: Chapter 1
**Payoff expected**: Chapter 3 (firmware modification confirmed — the colonies are being prevented from reporting)
**Urgency**: MEDIUM
**Plant**: "A rising cyano load and a flat, untroubled coherence reading was a sentence with no verb." The mycorrhizal colonies should be showing a suppression response if cyano is genuinely elevated. Their silence is biologically impossible — unless the coherence signal, not the cyano signal, is what's being falsified.
**Expected payoff**: In Ch. 3, when the firmware modification is identified, the mechanism becomes clear: the firmware masks specific chemical signatures "within a defined range." The coherence signal — the colonies' own response — is being suppressed in the reporting layer, making it appear the colonies haven't noticed the bloom. The "sentence with no verb" is the exact thing the firmware was designed to create.
**Status**: OPEN
