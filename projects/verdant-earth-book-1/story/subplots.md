## MAIN-01 — Biofilter Anomaly Investigation
**Type**: main plot
**Status**: OPEN
**Introduced**: Chapter 1
**Last active**: Chapter 1
**Summary**: Stations Seven, Eight, and Nine in the eastern inlet are showing a consistent pattern in raw sensor data — minor cyanobacteria elevation paired with a flat mycorrhizal coherence index that cannot both be accurate. The same pattern appears in both the Water Authority's own sensor network and the Accord's independently maintained feed, ruling out a calibration fault. The anomaly is suppressed somewhere between raw station data and the regional summary feed; the summary reports all three stations as fully nominal. Yara has filed a report and begun investigation. Del Marchetti has independently identified the same discrepancy from the Accord side.
**Next expected**: Chapter 2 — Del brings the Accord's full sixty-day raw feed for comparison; Yara's lab assay results arrive (samples EI-S7-2091-0605, ~15 hours remaining); Yara approaches Director Nwosu.

## MYST-01 — The Deleted Report
**Type**: mystery
**Status**: OPEN
**Introduced**: Chapter 1
**Last active**: Chapter 1
**Summary**: Yara filed anomaly report EI-2091-447 through the Authority's incident-management system, received a confirmation number, and wrote that number by hand in her field notebook. ## MYST-02 — The Suppression Mechanism
**Type**: mystery
**Status**: OPEN
**Introduced**: Chapter 1
**Last active**: Chapter 1
**Summary**: Raw station data for Stations 7–9 shows the anomaly pattern; the regional summary feed shows those stations as fully nominal. Yara traced one elevated reading by hand through the aggregation algorithm's processing log and watched it get reclassified into the noise floor — not deleted but folded in as if it were never a signal. The same trace repeated on two other readings with the same result. Del has established that the suppression is happening before data leaves the Authority's system (the Accord dashboard pulls from the Authority's summary submission). Working hypothesis at end of Chapter 1: aggregation error with a specific blind spot — but the deletion of Yara's report suggests something more deliberate. True mechanism (firmware modification in biotech monitoring nodes) is unknown to the characters at this stage.
**Next expected**: Chapter 3 — physical station inspection reveals a firmware sync tag anomaly; firmware modification as the vector is identified.

## CHAR-01 — Yara's Faith in Institutional Process
**Type**: character beat
**Status**: OPEN
**Introduced**: Chapter 1
**Last active**: Chapter 1
**Summary**: Yara's entire professional practice is built on doing things right — precise documentation, correct routing, evidence-supported claims, appropriate urgency flags. She explicitly trusts the process: she filed the report correctly, to the right person, with the right evidence, and expected in forty-eight hours someone would look and find the answer. The deletion of that report is the first direct attack on this trust. She responds not by panicking or escalating impulsively but by writing a backup into the hardware log — still working within what she can do, still methodical, but the foundation has shifted. The chapter ends with her looking at the empty queue and not changing anything about how she holds herself. The arc question: what happens to someone who believes in institutions when institutions are what she is up against?
**Next expected**: Chapter 2 — Nwosu meeting is the next test; professional sideline dressed as reassurance will deepen the crack; Yara must decide whether to keep working within channels or begin working around them.
