# Back-Cover Blurb — The Mycelial City

New Accra, 2091. The city works — and Yara Osei has spent ten years making sure it keeps working.

*The biofilter at Station Seven smelled right, which was the first thing wrong.*

Two readings that can't both be true. A report that vanishes before it reaches her supervisor's desk. A quiet, professional sideline dressed up as reassurance. Yara has built a career on trusting what the data tells her — and the data is telling her that someone inside the institution she has spent her life serving would rather an algal bloom turn chemically irreversible than let the truth surface on schedule.

She has four days. She has a stubborn ally who knows where every body in the Accord is buried, and whose own credentials are about to be frozen for the trouble. And she has one thing the suspension couldn't touch: a public record nobody can quietly disappear — if she's willing to use it, and willing to live with what happens after she does.

*The Mycelial City* is a procedural thriller about what it costs to tell the truth inside the institutions built to protect it — and about what's still worth protecting once the truth is finally out where everyone can see it.
