## Yara Osei

**Current emotional state**: Controlled unease deepening into something colder and more resolved. She has moved from unease-at-a-mystery to unease-at-a-system she once trusted and now cannot use. The surface remains professional baseline — she did not argue with Nwosu, she thanked her for her time in the literal sense, she held the warmth between them without weaponizing it. Underneath: faith in institutional process is not merely cracked now but falsified by evidence. She filed correctly; the system swallowed it and closed. She escalated to the right person; the right person rotated her off the area. She is not panicking. She is working with what remains.

**Current knowledge**: See knowledge.md. 47-day suppression start confirmed; two-tier deletion confirmed; coordinated suppression across both institutions confirmed; ~78h remaining on the bloom clock; Nwosu has seen her evidence and chosen her lane; Del is committed.

**Current situation**: Afternoon, Day 2. Off eastern inlet monitoring. Personal drive locked in kit bag — now the primary evidence store. Off-channel investigation committed. Chapter 3 task: deeper pattern analysis of the 47-day raw data.

**Relationships changed this chapter**:
- Del Marchetti: moved from active co-investigator (informal) to explicit partnership with stated terms. She named the situation plainly ("I'm telling you what I'm going to do. You can be in or you can be clear of it"), and he chose in. Trust is earned and tested, not assumed.
- Director Nwosu: relationship is now load-bearing in a different direction. The courtesy held throughout the meeting. Nwosu read the lab results as an engineer (eyes moving the way an engineer's eyes move) then responded as an operator. Three possible readings on Nwosu's position are all still valid: protecting process, protecting reputation, protecting herself.

**Arc position**: Act One, Chapter 2. The institutional escalation path has been tried and has closed. The faith tested in Chapter 1 (filing correctly) is now falsified (filing correctly and being rotated off for it). She is entering Chapter 3 without any legitimate channel and with a bloom clock running.

**Continuity notes for writer**:
- Crisis mode is stillness — she went very still over the deleted receipt, and she left Nwosu's office without argument
- She does not reach out first; she let Del come to her in Ch. 1, she sent a plain message in Ch. 2 ("can you come to me, or should I come to you?")
- The bloom calculation was worked through out loud — she thinks better explaining, and she was letting Del follow; this is a character beat to preserve
- Her final line of Ch. 2 is a statement of fact, not drama: "If someone deleted that report, they know what I found. They know I'll find it again." She said it because it was true, not for effect.
