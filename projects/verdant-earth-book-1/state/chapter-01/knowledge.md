## What Yara knows

### Confirmed facts
- Station Seven's cyanobacteria load has been intermittently elevated (minor, below notification threshold) on four separate occasions over the past three weeks
- In each case the mycorrhizal coherence index ran flat/nominal alongside the elevation — physically impossible if the colonies are responding correctly
- The discrepancy is patterned, not random: same type of pairing, same three stations (7, 8, 9), all eastern-inlet, all estuary-adjacent
- The anomaly is not appearing in the regional summary feed — the stations read fully nominal there
- She traced one anomalous reading through the aggregation algorithm by hand and watched it get reclassified into the noise floor (not deleted — smoothed flat, three steps repeated with the same result)
- The same pattern appears for all three affected stations; the other five eastern stations read clean in both raw data and summary
- Del's Accord monitoring network — independent hardware, independently maintained — sees the same three-station anomaly in raw data, also absent from the summary (which pulls from the Authority's submission)
- Therefore: the flattening is happening on the Authority's side before the data leaves their system
- Report EI-2091-447 was received by the incident management system at ~mid-morning, confirmation returned ("Routed to Director Nwosu for review. Estimated review window: 48 hours.")
- By evening (~nine hours later) the report has no record in the system — it does not appear under the report number, by date, by station reference, or in her submission history

### Suspected but unconfirmed
- Working hypothesis (still held): aggregation algorithm has an error — a blind spot that systematically misclassifies this specific chemical signature (cyano elevation + flat coherence) into the baseline
- Second branch (run but not concluded): someone with access to the system is choosing what not to pass on; Yara ran this branch to its logical form and noted that she has no answers — she is holding the question without acting on it
- The deletion of the report is now an additional data point; she has not yet attached it to a hypothesis about mechanism or actor

### Explicitly does NOT know
- Who deleted the report or how
- Whether Director Nwosu saw the report before it was deleted
- That the firmware in Stations 7, 8, 9 has been modified (this explains the flat coherence reading)
- That Altera Technical Services or Rational Innovation is involved
- That the bloom is already forming in the estuary
- The full historical depth of the anomaly (Del is pulling sixty-day history; she has thirty days)
