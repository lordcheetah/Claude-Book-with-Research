## Last chapter summary
Yara Osei conducts a pre-dawn routine inspection at Station Seven and finds two sensor readings that cannot both be true: a minor cyanobacteria elevation that the mycorrhizal coherence index isn't answering. She takes physical samples, logs a station flag, and submits a formal anomaly report (EI-2091-447) to Director Nwosu's queue. Late in the day, Del Marchetti arrives to compare notes — his independent Accord monitoring feed shows the same three-station pattern from a completely separate sensor network, ruling out a calibration fault. Together they establish that the data is being flattened before it leaves the Authority's system. That evening, Yara discovers her report has been deleted from the system with no record of submission. She writes a backup note directly to Station Seven's embedded hardware log.
