## Yara Osei

**Current emotional state**: Controlled unease. The surface is professional baseline — she has not panicked, has not acted out of proportion. Underneath: she found her report gone from a system she filed it with correctly, four hours after receiving confirmation. She is sitting with a shape she has not yet named. The sensory metaphor she uses for Del's arrival is "crackling air." The cold note underneath it is the thing she is not saying.

**Current knowledge**: See knowledge.md. In brief: the anomaly is real and patterned, confirmed by two independent networks; the summary feed is suppressing it; her official report was deleted; she doesn't know by whom or how.

**Current situation**: At home, courtyard, end of chapter. Has taken every available action within her reach. Waiting on lab results. Plans to meet Del tomorrow with his sixty-day historical pull.

**Relationships changed this chapter**:
- Del Marchetti: moved from "familiar colleague" to active co-investigator, unspoken. They compared raw feeds side by side on the staircase landing and reached the same structural conclusion without either of them saying the word "deliberate." The working relationship advanced. Warmth present but not stated.
- Director Nwosu: unchanged on-page; Yara routed the report to her in full professional trust. Nwosu did not appear in person. The deletion may eventually implicate someone with administrative access — ambiguity to be maintained.

**Arc position**: End of Act One, Chapter 1. Faith that the system will work has been directly tested and cracked — not broken, but cracked. She filed correctly, to the right person, with the right evidence. It did not work. She is entering Chapter 2 without a working escalation path.

**Continuity notes for writer**:
- Her crisis mode is stillness, not action — she "went very still" when the report disappeared
- She holds hypotheses until the data forces revision (still holding "possibly algorithm error" as the parsimonious frame, though privately she ran the deliberate branch too)
- She does not reach out first; she waits and assesses before opening up (this governed her behavior with Del)
- "Interesting" is a personal danger signal — has been deployed once; do not overuse

## Del Marchetti

**Current emotional state**: Alert, wry, not yet alarmed. He presented his data, confirmed coordination with Yara, landed the structural observation (*before it leaves the Authority's system*), and departed. The seriousness is present underneath the humor — his laugh at the end was "the laugh of a man who'd just been told something true." He is treating this as a real problem; he is not yet treating it as a crisis.

**Current knowledge**: See knowledge.md. He knows about the three-station anomaly from the Accord side; he knows the summary feed is clean when the raw data isn't; he knows the flattening is happening on the Authority's side before data leaves their system. He does not know the report was deleted (Yara discovered this after he left).

**Current situation**: Has left Yara's building. Committed to pulling Accord's full historical feed (all three stations, sixty days) for comparison tomorrow.

**Relationships changed this chapter**:
- Yara Osei: moved from "familiar colleague" to active co-investigator. They shared raw data directly and drew a structural conclusion together. Not yet intimate — the "dropped in unannounced" quality is explained by urgency and the inadequacy of calling for a "looking-at-screens thing." Relationship should warm slowly across subsequent chapters.

**Arc position**: Beginning — institutional ally taking the anomaly seriously, not yet committed to anything beyond the data comparison.

**Continuity notes for writer**:
- His Technist background has not been introduced and should not be surfaced yet
- "Here's the thing" is a canonical voice marker — used correctly in Ch. 1, don't overuse
- His hands sketch shapes in the air when he's thinking through problems out loud
- He reads institutional structure quickly and names it cleanly
