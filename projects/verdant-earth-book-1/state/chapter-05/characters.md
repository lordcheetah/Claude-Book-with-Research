## Yara Osei

**Current emotional state**: Past the stillness of crisis mode and into something quieter and harder — the state of someone who has done everything that can be done and is now simply waiting for the result. She went to Nwosu not to extract a confession but to put a fact in the room: *I know, and it's going to be seen.* She got exactly that said, exactly once, and let the silence be the answer. There is no triumph in it and no relief. She armed the upload and sat down to watch it, which is its own kind of admission — that the next move belongs to the system now, not to her.

**Current knowledge**: The Open Knowledge Compact mechanism — fully understood: routes to the public record, independently verifiable, triggers mandatory institutional response. Emil Strand named as the certifying analyst (negligence-vs-complicity still open). Has formed a private inference about Nwosu — that she authorized the deletion as a misguided judgment call, protecting process and reputation and, underneath both, herself — but holds this as inference, not fact, and has deliberately kept it out of the deposit package. Bloom clock: ~22h remaining at chapter close.

**Current situation**: Pre-dawn Day 4. Still locked out of all Authority systems; Compact deposit credentials active and untouched. Deposit package assembled and upload armed for 5 AM — has not yet executed. Awake, watching the countdown.

**Physical inventory (Yara)**:
- Paper notebook: EI-2091-447, "Not a misfile" note, hardware log reference numbers — unchanged
- Personal drive: now also holds the assembled deposit package (firmware delta, Altera records, hardware log photographs, Rational Innovation link, bloom projection, sourced access-tier description of the deletion) armed for scheduled upload at 5 AM
- Compact deposit credentials: ACTIVE, confirmed untouched this chapter (checked personally before the upload was armed)
- Field kit and physical samples: unchanged from prior chapters

**Credentials status**: Authority credentials remain SUSPENDED. Compact deposit credentials: ACTIVE and confirmed operational — used to arm the scheduled upload.

**Relationships changed this chapter**:
- Director Nwosu: the relationship crossed from institutional subordinate-to-superior into something starker — Yara confronted her directly, stated what she knew without accusing, and received silence in return. Nothing is resolved between them; Yara does not expect it to be. She has chosen not to carry an accusation into the package, which is its own form of restraint toward a woman she no longer fully trusts but does not believe is a villain.
- Del Marchetti: steady. He explained the Compact mechanism to the commons in his own voice, surfaced Emil Strand without overselling what it proves, and stood with her through the morning without crowding the moment that belonged to her alone.

**Arc position**: Act Two, chapter 2. She has moved from confirming the mechanism and the motive (Ch. 3–4) to acting entirely outside the institutions that failed her (Ch. 5). This chapter is the turn from *investigation* to *action with consequences she cannot control* — she has done the last thing she can do from outside the system, and now must wait to see whether the system, faced with something it cannot make disappear, responds the way the Compact promises it will.

**Continuity notes for writer**:
- She did not accuse Nwosu of anything on-page; she stated three facts in a row and left space after them — preserve this register going into Chapter 6
- Her private read of Nwosu (misguided judgment call, not corruption) is HERS — not corroborated, not stated aloud, not in the package; do not let Chapter 6 treat it as confirmed fact without new evidence
- The hand-to-notebook gesture under pressure (established Ch. 4) recurred this chapter — it is now a established physical tell under stress
- She checked her Compact credentials personally rather than trusting Del's assurance — this is her established discipline (photograph everything, verify everything herself) carrying through

## Del Marchetti

**Current emotional state**: Settled into something that looks like purpose rather than urgency. Explaining the Compact to the commons was the most "in his voice" he has been since the investigation began — institutional fluency turned toward something constructive rather than merely diagnostic. Naming Emil Strand cost him nothing performative; he was careful, precise, and explicit about the limits of what the signature proves. He is, for the first time in the book, doing the thing his particular skill set was always meant to do: translating a closed system into something the public can use against itself.

**Current knowledge**: Full understanding of the Compact mechanism (he is the one who explained it). Knows Emil Strand's employment history and the specifics of what he signed — and is explicit that this does not resolve whether Strand knew what he was certifying. Aware that Yara went to confront Nwosu alone and chose not to accompany her — respected that choice without commentary.

**Current situation**: Pre-dawn Day 4. Secondment still frozen; archive access still suspended. Personal notes, printed records, and his own copy of the assembled package remain his working materials. Present for the deposit's arming; will be present (presumably) for whatever the 5 AM upload brings.

**Physical inventory (Del)**:
- Printed certification records, notepad, personal notes — unchanged from Chapter 4
- New: notes on Emil Strand's employment history and the standards-division dismissal, gathered for the commons explanation and the package

**Credentials status**: Unchanged — secondment frozen, archive access suspended, personal materials retained.

**Relationships changed this chapter**:
- Yara Osei: he let her go to Nwosu alone. This is new — in every prior confrontation he has been present, talking, mapping the institutional terrain. Stepping back here is its own statement about what he understands the moment to require, and about what he now trusts her to do without him.

**Arc position**: Act Two, chapter 2. His Technist connection did not advance this chapter — he is, instead, visibly using the skills that connection gave him for the first time in service of something he chose, not something he was placed in. This is the quiet continuation of the "moral accounting" the bible notes for his arc, expressed through action rather than confession.

**Continuity notes for writer**:
- "Here's the thing" register preserved in his Compact explanation to the commons
- He did not go with Yara to Nwosu's office — a deliberate choice, worth tracking; do not retcon this as an oversight
- The Strand reveal is carefully bounded: "I don't know what he knew. I know what he signed." Preserve this exact distinction in Chapter 6 — the negligence-vs-complicity fork stays open
- His Technist subtext remains available but unforced; Chapter 6 can develop it further or let it stand as established texture

## Director Adaeze Nwosu

**Current emotional state**: Cornered without being accused — which, for a person who has spent a career managing what rises and what waits, may be worse than an accusation would have been. She tried to explain herself in the language of process and judgment calls, reached the edge of an actual admission ("I told myself —"), and stopped. She did not deny anything Yara laid in front of her. She did not ask Yara to stop. She said nothing at all to the one direct thing Yara told her.

**Current knowledge**: Knows that Yara has assembled something real, that it is going somewhere Nwosu cannot stop, and that Yara knows — or has inferred — enough about the deletion to lay it out as three connected facts without ever calling it an accusation. Whether she now understands what is about to happen to her is open.

**Current situation**: Water Authority Director. Confronted directly by Yara, pre-dawn Day 4. Did not move to stop the conversation, did not call for anyone, did not deny. Status and next move: unknown — she is now a person waiting for something she cannot prevent, much like Yara is.

**Arc position**: This chapter advances but does not resolve her three-reading ambiguity. The "I told myself —" is the closest the story has come to letting her speak from inside the thing she did, and it stops exactly at the point where confession would begin. The Yara-side interpretation (misguided judgment call) is now on the table as Yara's private read — but the text itself still permits all three readings (process-protector, reputation-protector, self-protector — possibly all three, layered).

**Continuity notes for writer**:
- Do NOT resolve her guilt or innocence definitively without new evidence — "I told myself —" is a plant, not a payoff; if Chapter 6 pays it off, it should do so through what she does next, not through a retroactive confession
- She did not deny the three facts Yara laid down (Director-level access, the rotation's origin) — this silence is itself information; preserve its weight
- "The warmth hadn't gone out of her, but something had settled into place behind it" (Ch. 2 characterization) — still true; this chapter adds "a person who has decided not to defend herself, which is not the same as admitting fault"
