# Timeline History — Seed Vault (Verdant Earth Book 2)

*Append-only archive of completed-chapter timelines.*

---

## Chapter 1: Lot 441

- [2094, October approx.] Maialèn Etxeberria flags Lot 441 at the Donostia vault — viability certificate 91.00% on departure and 91.00% on arrival at Q'asa Wasi, 11 months and 3 testing events later, with no drift
- [same day] Maialèn departs Donostia for Q'asa Wasi
- [same day + transit, ~16-18 hours] Maialèn arrives at Q'asa Wasi — meets Inés Huanca Quispe, inspects Lot 441's records, confirms the number is accurate but the intermediate re-test was skipped
- [end of chapter] Maialèn requests the monitoring-cycle records for Lot 441

---

## Chapter 2: The Monitoring Gap

- [Day 2, Q'asa Wasi] Inés produces monitoring-cycle record for Lot 441 — automated suspension flag, D-score 2.1 / threshold 2.5, Protocol 4.7(c)
- [Day 2, evening] Maialèn compiles D-scores for all 43 Q'asa Wasi outgoing lots (range 2.0–3.5); Atlantic circuit average 5.2, Mediterranean 5.1; gap >2 points
- [Day 3 morning] Morning germination check on Nursery Terraces; Maialèn asks Inés about D-score discrepancy; Inés responds "We are familiar with the scoring system"
- [Day 3 evening] Maialèn files escalation MEE-441-2094-Q to Atlantic Seed Trust: monitoring-gap + D-score calibration question
- [Day 4 morning] Trust response arrives: "no further action recommended at this time"
- [Day 4 evening] Maialèn asks Inés for the cooperative's own pre-Covenant germination ledgers; Inés says "Come back in the morning"

---

## Chapter 3: The Old Ledgers

- [Day 5 morning] Inés redirects Maialèn from the records room to a full day of tuber harvest on the Long Terraces — an implicit character test
- [Day 5 morning–midday] Maialèn works the harvest with Rosalía's crew, struggles at first (spears several tubers), gradually improves
- [Day 5 afternoon] On a separate terrace, Doña Encarnación (forty-one years on her own tuber line) tells Maialèn: "The paper says what the paper was given. I know what this plant does."
- [Day 5 afternoon, records room] Inés opens the locked records cabinet — pre-Covenant germination ledgers, some predating the 1990s
- [Day 5 afternoon] Maialèn cross-references 15 lots over 9 years from the ledgers against official D-scores: germination in the low-to-mid 90s%, viability 85%+, against D-scores of 2.0–3.5 — names the pattern "too tidy" (a methodology correctly and consistently applied to the wrong plant)
- [Day 5 evening] Inés reveals "eleven years": in 2083 the GDPC ran a methodology review of the 2079 comparison-set framework; Q'asa Wasi submitted three comments (including a high-altitude comparator request with supporting data), all received/acknowledged/not incorporated; the framework was adopted unchanged in 2084; Maialèn's escalation two days ago makes it four attempts total
- [Day 5 evening] Maialèn asks who designed the original 2079 methodology; Inés says the cooperative has never had a name, only the document
- [Day 5, past midnight] Maialèn reads the GDPC 2079 methodology archive in her room — finds the authorship appendix, an eleven-person working-group roster, and begins searching it for a connection

## Chapter 4: The Subpanel

- [Day 5, past midnight, continued] Maialèn re-reads the GDPC 2079 working-group roster, shifts her question from "who's on this list" to "where did the original numbers come from" — finds the authorship appendix's task assignments
- [Day 5, past midnight] Maialèn traces the design history: six of the eleven did the technical drafting (three from commercial-crop cooperatives, one from a certification-readiness advisory body, two methodology statisticians); the founding dataset was a 2071–2076 multi-region survey covering 41 bioregions, none above 2,000m
- [Day 5, near dawn] Maialèn closes the notebook, sleeps a few hours
- [Day 6, dawn] Maialèn finds Inés in the nursery terraces; reports the design history. Inés notes this answers "who built this," not "who can fix it" — a different question
- [Day 6 through Day 8 morning] Maialèn continues working the GDPC archive (off-page)
- [Day 8, morning] Bixente arrives via supply truck on the pass road, exactly on his own schedule — secures his cargo before greeting Maialèn (established habit)
- [Day 8, morning] Bixente learns Maialèn filed the Trust escalation (MEE-441-2094-Q, filed Day 3) without telling him first; both acknowledge it plainly — resolved as "square"
- [Day 8, midday] Bixente reviews all of Maialèn's findings (ledger cross-reference, 43-lot circuit comparison, design history) at the records table
- [Day 8, midday] Bixente spots a new pattern: the same andigenum varietal scores 4.6 via the Mediterranean circuit vs. 2.3 via the Q'asa Wasi circuit — a geographic bias layered beneath the taxonomic mismatch
- [Day 8, midday] Maialèn's air-sketching gesture returns (absent since Donostia/Chapter 2); she names the new pattern "too narrow" — third evolution of "too clean" → "too tidy" → "too narrow" (narrow dataset, narrow working group, geographically-patterned harm)
- [Day 8, midday] Maialèn locates the current Comparison-Set Methodology Subpanel (GDPC Technical Council standing body, est. 2080, six members, four with commercial-crop-development ties)
- [Day 8, midday] Maialèn identifies a continuity link: one of the original eleven (advisory side) now also sits on the Subpanel — fourteen years of institutional continuity
- [Day 8, midday] A loose, unconfirmed thread surfaces: one Subpanel member's consortium affiliation (Mediterranean basin) triggers a vague half-memory for Maialèn; Bixente recalls "a colleague of a colleague" who left a consortium post "around the time of that business in New Accra" — written down, not pursued
- [Day 8, midday] Maialèn notices a GDPC Technical Council session listed for "next month" — not investigated, set aside
- [Day 8, midday] Inés finds Maialèn and Bixente at the records table; Maialèn asks Q'asa Wasi to formally co-file a methodology challenge alongside her findings
- [Day 8, midday] The three move to the Watching Wall; Inés acknowledges the real stakes of a formal co-filing (council vote, communal risk) and agrees to bring the request to the council that evening — "We have been waiting for someone to ask that question for eleven years." Council decision PENDING.

## Chapter 5: Outside the Scope

- [Day 9, dawn] Inés and Tomás (a retired, 30-year council member) bring Maialèn the result of the previous night's vote: the council called in the full assembly (the five-member standing council, plus terrace representatives and elders who've served before) — 8 in favor, 3 against. Q'asa Wasi formally co-files, by name, alongside Maialèn; Inés delivers a handwritten, sealed council authorization document.
- [Day 9, dawn] Tomás voices his dissent on record: a good-faith warning about non-overt institutional retaliation (slowed grants, rescheduled monitoring visits — "an institution that has, very quietly, stopped being in a hurry about you"). Inés confirms the council heard this and chose to proceed anyway, "eyes open."
- [Day 9, ~20 minutes later] The Trust's portal delivers a closure letter (*Re: Field Assignment MEE-2094-QW-04*): the Lot 441 monitoring-gap is "adequately explained," no fraud found, and the methodology question is "a policy matter... outside the scope of routine audit review." Maialèn's field assignment is "considered complete, effective immediately"; interim report due within 7 days.
- [Day 9] Maialèn's "let me say it out loud" counting habit reaches "One... Two..." and stops — no third item arrives. She and Bixente name this explicitly: a decision, not a finding. She chooses to continue under her own credentials.
- [Days 9-12] Four-day compilation period: Maialèn drafts a four-part formal methodology challenge document (scoring methodology / "too tidy" mismatch; Protocol 4.7(c) monitoring-frequency mechanism; 15-lot/9-year ledger cross-reference + "too narrow" geographic gap; regulatory timeline). Bixente fact-checks every figure; Inés circulates drafts to younger council members for tone.
- [Day 10, "second morning"] Maialèn investigates the GDPC Technical Council session previously noted only as "next month" — finds it's the Comparison-Set Methodology Subpanel's codification-recommendation session, ten days out from this point, the last step before the Council votes to make the current framework binding. Writes the Subpanel into the document by full composition (6 members, 4 with commercial-crop ties, 1 with 14-year continuity to the 2079 working group).
- [Day 10] The "New Accra" half-memory gets a brief, neutral mention in the document — the Mediterranean-basin Subpanel member's affiliation, same one-line treatment as the other five. Thread remains unconfirmed.
- [Day 11, "third afternoon"] Yenny (a younger council member who runs the archive interface) uploads the pre-Covenant ledgers (15 lots / 9 years) to the Compact's open agricultural archive — reference *QW-GERM-2079-2093, archived*. Independent citation count climbs 0 → 1 (at 19 minutes) → 2 → 3.
- [Day 11, evening] Document complete except for the Lot 441 backup-stock trial placeholder (scheduled Day 12); timeline in the document set against the session, now ten days out.
- [Day 12, dawn] Germination trial: Lot 441's backup stock tests at 78% against the certified 91% — a confirmed 13-point loss. Maialèn's hand twitches toward the air-sketching gesture but she stops herself (negative callback — this is a feared number arriving, not a pattern to discover). Inés names it "real loss... while the system that should have caught it was busy not catching it."
- [Day 12, morning] Backup-stock mobilization: held-back stock redistributed across three terraces within the hour, drilled and communal, never called a drill. Doña Encarnación briefly sighted on the Long Terraces, already moving a tray into position.
- [Day 12, evening] The trial result is added to the document's fourth section. Document complete: four parts, every figure cited, Subpanel named, timeline set against a session now nine days out.
- [Day 12, night] Watching Wall: Maialèn names her feeling "grief," not despair, for the lot's loss. Bixente joins silently, confirms the document and filing are ready ("Then it's ready").
- [Day 12, 04:00] Maialèn files a Rights Tribunal petition under her own Atlantic Seed Trust credentials (registration AST-7734) as petitioner, with the Q'asa Wasi Cooperative Seed Council (represented by Inés) as co-petitioner. Confirmation: reference number, timestamp "04:00, Day 12," *"Submission received. Under review."*

## Chapter 6: The Record

- [Day 12, dawn] At the Watching Wall, the Tribunal's Notice of Acceptance arrives (Ref. RT-2094-11847): the petition filed at 04:00 is accepted for expedited review under Section 9; a case officer to be assigned within five working days.
- [Day 12, dawn, same notice] An administrative hold (Notice AH-2094-0231) is issued on the GDPC Technical Council's session — the Comparison-Set Methodology Subpanel's codification-recommendation presentation is postponed pending independent review of the scoring methodology, with no date set for rescheduling.
- [Day 12, dawn] Bixente reports the ledger archive's citation count on QW-GERM-2079-2093 has gone from three (last night) to fourteen (this morning) — three bioregions now querying independently (Atlantic, Mediterranean, and an Andean group).
- [Day 12, dawn] Maialèn's "let me say it out loud" counting habit completes cleanly for the first time: petition accepted, Subpanel session on hold, citation count climbing across three bioregions — nothing missing, nothing in it frightening her.
- [Day 12, mid-morning, records room] The Trust sends a registration-status message: Maialèn's Tribunal filing under her own credentials (AST-7734) has triggered a review of her registration status — "under review pending consultation with the Compact's standards office. No action is required from you at this time." Maialèn writes herself a note: "AST-7734: under review. Filed anyway. Would file again."
- [Day 12, midday] Planting day on the Long Terraces: Maialèn works alongside Inés, Rosalía, and Doña Encarnación, learning the foot-plow; Doña Encarnación corrects her planting depth ("Two knuckles. The frost gets the shallow ones first").
- [Day 12, midday] Inés tells Maialèn that the rest of the redistributed backup stock is going to a cooperative two valleys over, whose own backup stock came back under 30% viability and whose heritage oca line is effectively lost — what remains is being outcrossed with a related variety using Q'asa Wasi's donated surplus.
- [Day 12, midday] Maialèn's enumeration habit runs a five-item "partial win" recap mid-planting — including the two-valleys-over lot as a real ending, distinct from Q'asa Wasi's recoverable 13-point loss — and names this "what a partial win looks like."
- [Day 12, late afternoon, records room] Maialèn writes and signs her final audit report for Field Assignment MEE-2094-QW-04: three findings (the monitoring-suspension mechanism operated as designed; the comparison-set methodology itself is the structural flaw, fifteen years uncorrected; the Lot 441 backup-stock trial — 78% vs. 91%, a 13-point loss — is direct evidence of real-world harm) plus one open question (whether the eventual review extends beyond the Subpanel's current composition to the broader geographic-bias pattern).
- [Day 12, late afternoon] Inés relays that Tomás heard the day's news before they came down from the Wall: glad to have been wrong about the timing, not the principle. Maialèn asks Inés to tell him she's glad he spoke plainly in the room before the vote.
- [Day 12, evening] Watching Wall: Inés tells the story of her grandmother's 1991 ledger entry — written in pencil, in an unofficial notebook, during the cooperative's worst years, with no expectation of an audience: "Someone has to remember what grew here." The Tuesday supply truck arrives. Maialèn writes a few private lines in her own notebook.
- [Three weeks later, Donostia — closing scene] The Tribunal review remains in early stages (case officer assigned as promised; a documentation request answered same-day; no findings yet). The Subpanel's session remains undated. The QW-GERM-2079-2093 citation count has climbed past forty. Maialèn's registration status (AST-7734) has updated to "under continued review, no further action required at this time." Her interim report was filed on schedule and received without comment. Maialèn rereads the original Lot 441 transfer record and writes a margin note beside the arrival figure, in the space left blank five weeks ago: "Whose claim? Mine now too. I signed it forward. — M.E.G." The book ends with Maialèn closing the notebook and satchel, drinking her coffee while it's still warm.
