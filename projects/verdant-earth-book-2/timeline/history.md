## Chapter 1: Lot 441

- [2094, October approx.] Maialèn Etxeberria flags Lot 441 at the Donostia vault — viability certificate 91.00% on departure and 91.00% on arrival at Q'asa Wasi, 11 months and 3 testing events later, with no drift
- [same day] Maialèn departs Donostia for Q'asa Wasi
- [same day + transit, ~16-18 hours] Maialèn arrives at Q'asa Wasi — meets Inés Huanca Quispe, inspects Lot 441's records, confirms the number is accurate but the intermediate re-test was skipped
- [end of chapter] Maialèn requests the monitoring-cycle records for Lot 441

## Chapter 2: The Monitoring Gap

- [Day 2, Q'asa Wasi] Inés produces monitoring-cycle record for Lot 441 — automated suspension flag, D-score 2.1 / threshold 2.5, Protocol 4.7(c)
- [Day 2, evening] Maialèn compiles D-scores for all 43 Q'asa Wasi outgoing lots (range 2.0–3.5); Atlantic circuit average 5.2, Mediterranean 5.1; gap >2 points
- [Day 3 morning] Morning germination check on Nursery Terraces; Maialèn asks Inés about D-score discrepancy; Inés responds "We are familiar with the scoring system"
- [Day 3 evening] Maialèn files escalation MEE-441-2094-Q to Atlantic Seed Trust: monitoring-gap + D-score calibration question
- [Day 4 morning] Trust response arrives: "no further action recommended at this time"
- [Day 4 evening] Maialèn asks Inés for the cooperative's own pre-Covenant germination ledgers; Inés says "Come back in the morning"

## Chapter 3: The Old Ledgers

- [Day 5 morning] Inés redirects Maialèn from the records room to a full day of tuber harvest on the Long Terraces — an implicit character test
- [Day 5 morning–midday] Maialèn works the harvest with Rosalía's crew, struggles at first (spears several tubers), gradually improves
- [Day 5 afternoon] On a separate terrace, Doña Encarnación (forty-one years on her own tuber line) tells Maialèn: "The paper says what the paper was given. I know what this plant does."
- [Day 5 afternoon, records room] Inés opens the locked records cabinet — pre-Covenant germination ledgers, some predating the 1990s
- [Day 5 afternoon] Maialèn cross-references 15 lots over 9 years from the ledgers against official D-scores: germination in the low-to-mid 90s%, viability 85%+, against D-scores of 2.0–3.5 — names the pattern "too tidy" (a methodology correctly and consistently applied to the wrong plant)
- [Day 5 evening] Inés reveals "eleven years": in 2083 the GDPC ran a methodology review of the 2079 comparison-set framework; Q'asa Wasi submitted three comments (including a high-altitude comparator request with supporting data), all received/acknowledged/not incorporated; the framework was adopted unchanged in 2084; Maialèn's escalation two days ago makes it four attempts total
- [Day 5 evening] Maialèn asks who designed the original 2079 methodology; Inés says the cooperative has never had a name, only the document
- [Day 5, past midnight] Maialèn reads the GDPC 2079 methodology archive in her room — finds the authorship appendix, an eleven-person working-group roster, and begins searching it for a connection
