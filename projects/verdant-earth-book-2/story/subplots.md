### The Lot 441 Anomaly
- **Type**: mystery/question
- **Introduced**: Chapter 01 — Maialèn notices that Lot 441 carried an identical viability reading of 91.00% across all four certification events spanning eleven months and two independent intermediate testing stations (Lisbon and Guayaquil), which is statistically impossible if the retests were actually performed.
- **Resolved**: Chapter 05 — The diagnostic mystery (the "too clean" → "too tidy" → "too narrow" progression, Ch1-4) culminates in a direct empirical test: Q'asa Wasi's archived backup-stock sample for Lot 441 is germinated and counted under direct observation. Result: **78% viability against the certified 91% — a confirmed loss of thirteen percentage points**, consistent with the documented suppression pattern. The loss is real, not a methodology artifact, and is absorbed via the cooperative's backup-stock reallocation (a successful, communal, non-panicked mobilization). The figure is entered as the final piece of the four-part methodology challenge document.
- **Assessment**: Earned, and structurally significant. This converts what had been (across four chapters) an entirely paper-and-ledger investigation into a confirmed real-world harm with a concrete number — the chapter's emotional turning point ("Grief, maybe... a system designed to notice did not notice"). Per the chapter-05 plan's carry-forward notes, this resolved instance (recovered via backup stock) is explicitly held in reserve as a contrast point for Chapter 6's "not all lots are recoverable" framing — the diagnostic thread closes here, but its consequence (this specific 13-point loss) now lives on as evidence within the new Rights Tribunal Petition thread.

- **Type**: character arc beat
- **Introduced**: Chapter 01 — Maialèn is an experienced, methodical auditor eighteen years in; she arrived at Q'asa Wasi on what should be a routine reconciliation. The chapter ends with her opening her satchel notebook to a clean page — committed, but not yet knowing what she is committed to.
