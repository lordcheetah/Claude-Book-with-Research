- **The cooperative two valleys over (NEW, Day 12, midday):** A neighboring cooperative whose D-score suppression persisted longer than Q'asa Wasi's. Their own backup stock came back under 30% viability last month. Their heritage oca line — kept "unbroken" by generations of the cooperative's keepers — is effectively lost; what remains is being outcrossed with a related variety to preserve as much of the genetic material as possible, but the resulting plant "will be related. It won't be the same plant." Some of Q'asa Wasi's redistributed backup stock (from Chapter 5's mobilization) is being sent to help the cross take. This is explicitly framed as the contrast case to Q'asa Wasi's own loss: "the difference between a loss and an ending."
- **Final audit report — Field Assignment MEE-2094-QW-04 (NEW, Day 12, late afternoon, signed):**
  - *Finding 1:* The original anomaly in Lot 441's chain-of-custody (four identical 91.00% readings across eleven months and three testing events) was caused by the automated monitoring-cycle suspension mechanism (Protocol 4.7(c)), triggered by a D-score below threshold (2.1 vs. 2.5), and the mechanism operated as designed. No falsification by any individual at any stage.
  - *Finding 2:* The D-score itself is calculated against a comparison-set methodology (GDPC's 2079 framework) that classifies high-altitude Andean varietals — including Lot 441's variety — against a comparison set composed entirely of lowland commercial cultivars. Unrevisited since adoption. Resulting Q'asa Wasi D-scores (2.0–3.5) are systematically lower than scores for comparable varietals under more appropriate comparison sets (e.g., 4.6 for the same andigenum varietal under Mediterranean-circuit assessment vs. 2.3 for Q'asa Wasi).
  - *Finding 3:* This methodological gap produced measurable, material loss: the Lot 441 backup-stock germination trial (Day 12) returned 78% vs. the certified 91% — a confirmed 13-point degradation over an eleven-month transit period during which no re-testing occurred, demonstrating the comparison-set methodology's effect is not merely statistical but has real-world consequences for genetic stock under its jurisdiction.
  - *Open question:* Whether the independent review triggered by the Tribunal petition will examine only the Comparison-Set Methodology Subpanel's current composition, or will extend to the broader pattern of geographic bias this case suggests — affecting other high-altitude and heritage-variety collections across the network. Not yet known; not within this report's scope to determine.
  - Signed: name, registration number, date.
- **Inés's grandmother's 1991 ledger entry (NEW — CANDIDATE FOR SERIES BIBLE):** Inés's grandmother kept the cooperative's ledgers for forty years (her mother before her). The first entry in the grandmother's hand is dated 1991 — "the worst years" — a germination count for one of the tuber lines, recorded in pencil in a notebook that was not yet the cooperative's official record (the official ledger had been lost the previous year, in "the resettlement"). No one asked her to record it; there was no archive, nothing public, nothing anyone outside the valley would ever see. When young Inés asked why she bothered, her grandmother said: "Someone has to remember what grew here." This page is now part of the archive deposited in Chapter 5 (QW-GERM-2079-2093). **This is new family/cooperative history, established for the first time in the final chapter — flag as a strong candidate for inclusion in the series bible** (establishes: the cooperative experienced a "resettlement" and a lost official ledger circa 1990; Inés's grandmother and great-grandmother as named-but-unnamed historical keepers; the thematic throughline of unwitnessed recordkeeping as an act of care).
- **Donostia closing scene (NEW, three weeks later):** Maialèn rereads the original Lot 441 transfer record — Departure (Donostia): 91.00%, Lisbon retest: 91.00%, Guayaquil retest: 91.00%, Arrival (Q'asa Wasi): 91.00% — the same four numbers that opened the book. She writes in the margin beside the arrival figure (left blank five weeks earlier): *"Whose claim? Mine now too. I signed it forward. — M.E.G."* — closing the book's central image and resolving the "whose claim is this?" question first posed in Chapter 1/2.
