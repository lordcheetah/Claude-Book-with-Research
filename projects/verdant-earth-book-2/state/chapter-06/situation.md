# Situation at End of Chapter 6

## Time
Day 12 of Maialèn's stay at Q'asa Wasi, dawn through dusk, October 2094 — then a hard cut to "three weeks later," October 2094, in Donostia. (Chapter spans Day 12 dawn through Day 12 night, then jumps approximately three weeks forward to the book's closing scene. The book ends roughly five weeks after Chapter 1's Day 1.)

## Location
The Donostia office, Atlantic Seed Trust — late afternoon, three weeks after returning from Q'asa Wasi. (Final scene of the book.)

## Tension Level
2 / 10 — Down from Chapter 5's 6/10. This is the book's resolution/landing chapter. Every active thread either resolves cleanly, settles into a stable "ongoing but no longer urgent" state, or is explicitly reframed as a long-horizon question for the series. The chapter's emotional register moves from relief through companionable groundedness to grief-without-despair (the two-valleys-over loss) to quiet resolve, landing on a settled, soft close — the kind of ending that closes a book without pretending every institutional question is answered.

## What Has Just Happened
- [Day 12, dawn] At the Watching Wall, the Tribunal's Notice of Acceptance arrives (Ref. RT-2094-11847): the petition is accepted for expedited review under Section 9, a case officer to be assigned within five working days.
- [Day 12, dawn, same notice] An administrative hold (Notice AH-2094-0231) is issued on the GDPC Technical Council Subpanel's session — the codification presentation/vote is postponed pending independent review of the methodology's scoring criteria, with no date set for rescheduling. The "nine-day countdown" that drove Chapter 5 is gone — not resolved, but stopped.
- [Day 12, dawn] Bixente reports the ledger archive's citation count has gone from three (last night) to fourteen (this morning) — three bioregions now querying independently (Atlantic, Mediterranean, and an Andean group).
- [Day 12, dawn] Maialèn's "let me say it out loud" counting habit completes cleanly for the first time in the book: three items (petition accepted; Subpanel session on hold; citation count climbing across three bioregions), nothing missing, nothing frightening.
- [Day 12, mid-morning, records room] The Trust sends a registration-status message regarding AST-7734: Maialèn's Tribunal filing under her own credentials has triggered a review of her registration status, "under review pending consultation with the Compact's standards office. No action is required from you at this time." Open-ended, unresolved — distinct from (and heavier than) the earlier field-assignment closure. Maialèn writes herself a note: *"AST-7734: under review. Filed anyway. Would file again."*
- [Day 12, midday] Planting day on the Long Terraces: Maialèn works alongside Inés, Rosalía, and Doña Encarnación, learning the foot-plow (Doña Encarnación's "two knuckles" depth correction). Some of the redistributed backup stock from three days ago goes into new ground.
- [Day 12, midday] Inés tells Maialèn that the rest of the redistributed backup stock is going to a cooperative two valleys over — their D-score suppression went on longer, their own backup stock came back under 30% viability, and their heritage oca line (kept "unbroken" for generations) is effectively lost; what remains is being outcrossed with a related variety to save what genetics can be saved. Q'asa Wasi's surplus will help the cross take, but won't restore the original line.
- [Day 12, midday] Maialèn's enumeration habit runs a five-item "partial win" recap mid-planting: (1) methodology review open, case officer pending; (2) codification session delayed, no new date; (3) citation count climbing across three bioregions, independent of the Tribunal; (4) the two-valleys-over lot — a real ending, not just a loss, distinguished explicitly from Q'asa Wasi's recoverable 13-point loss; (5) the Subpanel's composition unchanged today, full Tribunal review months-to-years away. She names this "what a partial win looks like."
- [Day 12, late afternoon, records room] Maialèn writes her formal closing audit report for Field Assignment MEE-2094-QW-04 — three findings (the monitoring-suspension mechanism operated as designed; the comparison-set methodology itself is the structural flaw, fifteen years uncorrected; the Lot 441 backup-stock trial — 78% vs. 91%, a 13-point loss — is direct, measurable evidence of real-world harm) plus one open question (whether the eventual review will examine only the Subpanel's current composition, or the broader geographic-bias pattern across the network). She signs it: name, registration number, date.
- [Day 12, late afternoon] Inés relays that Tomás heard the day's news before they came down from the Wall: he's "glad to have been wrong about the timing, if not the principle" — he still believes the cost was real, he just didn't expect a response this fast. Maialèn asks Inés to tell him she's glad he spoke plainly in the room before the vote.
- [Day 12, evening] Watching Wall: Inés tells the story of her grandmother's 1991 ledger entry — written in pencil, in an unofficial notebook, during the cooperative's worst years, with no audience and no expectation it would ever be seen, "because someone has to remember what grew here." The Tuesday supply truck arrives. Maialèn writes a few private lines in her own notebook (not shared, not read aloud).
- [Three weeks later, Donostia — closing scene] The Tribunal review remains in early stages (case officer assigned as promised; a documentation request answered same-day; no findings yet, none expected for a long time). The Subpanel's session remains undated. The QW-GERM-2079-2093 citation count has climbed past forty. Maialèn's registration status (AST-7734) has been updated to "under continued review, no further action required at this time." Her interim report was filed on schedule and received without comment. Maialèn rereads the original Lot 441 transfer record (the four identical 91.00% readings) and writes a margin note beside the arrival figure, in the space she'd left blank five weeks ago: *"Whose claim? Mine now too. I signed it forward. — M.E.G."* The book ends on this image — Maialèn closing the notebook and satchel, drinking her coffee while it's still warm, the Bay of Biscay outside the window.
