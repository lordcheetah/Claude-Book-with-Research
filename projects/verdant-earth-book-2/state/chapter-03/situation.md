# Situation at End of Chapter 3

## Time
Day 5 of Maialèn's stay at Q'asa Wasi, past midnight, October 2094.

## Location
Q'asa Wasi Vault House, Maialèn's room.

## Tension Level
5 / 10 — Not an escalation. A quieter, colder recalibration. Maialèn has moved from believing she'd found something new to understanding she's inherited an old, accurately-described problem that institutions have been failing to act on for over a decade.

## What Has Just Happened
- Inés redirected Maialèn's morning: no ledgers, instead a full day of tuber harvest on the Long Terraces with the work crew — an implicit character test. Maialèn went without complaint, worked the harvest (badly at first, then adequately), and passed.
- Doña Encarnación, an elder with forty-one years on her own tuber line, told Maialèn directly: "The paper says what the paper was given. I know what this plant does."
- That afternoon, Inés opened the locked records cabinet — pre-Covenant ledgers, some predating the 1990s, kept continuously by the cooperative.
- Maialèn cross-referenced 15 lots across 9 years from these ledgers against official D-scores: germination rates in the low-to-mid 90s%, viability above 85%, every time — against official D-scores of 2.0–3.5. She named the pattern "too tidy": a methodology that is internally consistent and correctly applied, but to the wrong plant. (An evolution of her earlier "too clean" / "Demasiado limpio" finding — that was an absence of expected drift; this is a methodology consistently misapplied.)
- That evening, Inés revealed "eleven years": in 2083 the Genetic Diversity Preservation Council ran a methodology review of the comparison-set framework. Q'asa Wasi submitted three comments over that review — including a high-altitude comparator request with supporting data — all received, acknowledged, and not incorporated. The 2079 framework was adopted unchanged in 2084. Maialèn's escalation two days ago makes it four attempts total.
- Maialèn asked who designed the original 2079 comparison-set methodology. Inés didn't know — only the document. After dinner, Maialèn pulled up the GDPC's public methodology archive and is now, past midnight, working through its authorship appendix: an eleven-person working-group roster, looking for a name or connection that tells her where to go next.

## Open Hooks
- The GDPC 2079 methodology archive's authorship appendix — eleven names, affiliations, dates of service. Maialèn is searching this roster for a connection (sets up Chapter 4's subpanel-composition reveal).
- Bixente arriving in approximately three days.
- The 2083 review was the framework's only substantive review cycle; nothing since. Eleven years of silence on both sides.
