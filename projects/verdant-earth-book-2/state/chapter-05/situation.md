Day 12 of Maialèn's stay at Q'asa Wasi, 04:00 (pre-dawn), October 2094. (Chapter spans Day 9 dawn through Day 12, 04:00.)

Inés confirms the council heard this and chose to proceed anyway, "eyes open."

- [Day 9] Maialèn's "let me say it out loud" counting habit reaches "One... Two..." and stops — no third item arrives. She and Bixente name this explicitly: this is a decision she has to make, not a finding to complete. She chooses to keep going, on her own credentials.

- [Day 11, evening] Document complete except for one placeholder: *[Pending: Lot 441 backup-stock germination trial, scheduled Day 12.]* Timeline in the document set against the GDPC session, now ten days out.

Result: 78% viability vs. 91% certified — a confirmed 13-point loss. Maialèn's hand twitches toward the air-sketching gesture but she stops herself — this is not a pattern to discover, it's a number she'd been afraid of, arriving.
